   Qwen3-RLVF V4 scores remain 0.826–0.992 on 5-domain data (vs 1.000
   in-domain). Base accuracy drops more severely (1.000→0.557), suggesting
   variant robustness is more transferable than base reasoning.

   Zero improvement from CoVe when model already trained correctly.

6. OOD limitation is honest and clearly scoped:
   Formal logic → natural language NLI gap is expected and documented.
